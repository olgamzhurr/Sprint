FAQ

Common questions.

Where do the sessions take place?
Sessions are held in person in Washington and California, but you're welcome to join online from anywhere. We run hybrid sessions so remote participants get the same experience as those in the room.
What if I miss a session?
Every session is recorded and available to watch. That said, the Sprint is designed around participation — you need to actively work on your business each day to see results. If you miss a session and don't catch up quickly, you risk falling behind the cohort. There are no shortcuts to the outcome.
Can I get a detailed program before signing up?
Yes. Share your email and you'll receive the daily task outline — the structure of what we cover each day. Note that this doesn't include the detailed content, industry best practices, or facilitation frameworks we use in the live sessions, but it gives you a clear picture of the process and what to expect.
Do I need prior business experience?
No. The Sprint is designed for anyone with an idea — whether you're starting from scratch or already have something in progress. What matters is that you're ready to commit the time and actually do the work.
What kind of business can I bring?
Any kind. Product, service, online, local, B2B, B2C — the framework works across business types. You bring the idea, we bring the structure.
